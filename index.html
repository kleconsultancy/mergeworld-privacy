🌸 MergeWorld - Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2025

Thank you for playing MergeWorld. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our mobile game.

1. Information We Collect

MergeWorld stores game progress data (score, level, game board state, coins, energy) locally on your device using Android SharedPreferences. This data is not transmitted to any server.

2. Third-Party Services

MergeWorld uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect certain information to provide personalized ads, including:

  • Device identifiers
  • IP address
  • App usage data

For more information about how Google uses data, please visit: https://policies.google.com/privacy

3. Data Storage

All game data is stored locally on your device. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information to external servers.

4. Children's Privacy

MergeWorld is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information, please contact us.

5. Advertising

MergeWorld displays ads through Google AdMob. You can opt out of personalized advertising by adjusting your device settings under Google Settings > Ads > Opt out of Ads Personalization.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
